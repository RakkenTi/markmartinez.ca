## Premise

The idea of the game is simple. One player has a portal gun, and the other has a physics gun. Together they must work as a pair to navigate through the obby stages. The map takes place in a blocky terrain based environment.

While the two players are provided with rather powerful tools, the game is still very challenging. It is quite a bit more difficult than most obbies, especially as players progress to the later stages.

## Results

I've personally found the game to be quite a success, given that it was the first commercial game I've published.
- In an overwhelmingly saturated market, the concept was unique enough to stand out from others, and was refined enough via many iterations to prevent easy copies from being made and published.
- As the game was released by me as a solo project, I knew every corner of the game and was able to iterate rapidly on the initial launch to solve any bugs or issues that came up in the live game, as a result, several critical bugs were fixed quickly during launch and the game eventually got picked up by the algorithm.
- The map is massive and spans between multiple biomes, starting off relatively easy and becoming increasingly difficult, satisfying both young players and more experienced players alike. This can be seen in the game's badge distribution, with difficulty spiking after the Sakura Skies biome.

## Reception
As this was a game with a rather short development cycle, I was not expecting to see such numbers: Over ten million visits and more than 75,000 favourites. Around 45,000 likes against 2,300 dislikes, a ratio a little above 95 percent. It has only been updated once, around the first month during release.
